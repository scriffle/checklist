Each radar chart shows a student's learning profile across six axes. The further a point reaches toward the outer edge, the stronger that metric is relative to the class. A value at the centre means the student is at the class minimum for that metric; the outer ring means class maximum. The raw values (e.g. 86%, 92 level-ups, 25 learning points) are shown at each data point so you can assess absolute performance. For Perseverance, each chart also shows how the student's questions and active time compare to the class median (e.g. "+20q, +5m" means 20 more questions and 5 more active minutes than the typical student). The Class Summary panel shows the class median and full range for questions and time, so you can judge whether the class baseline itself is high or low. Key relationship — Accuracy vs Mastery: Accuracy is the proportion correct across all attempts. Mastery is accuracy in the student's most recent attempts only. When both are high, the student understood from the start. When Accuracy is lower than Mastery, the student has built understanding over time — that's real learning in action. When Accuracy is higher than Mastery, there is an opportunity to revisit recent material and rebuild momentum. Below are common profile shapes you may see, what they suggest, and how you might respond.

Accuracy

Proportion of questions answered correctly

Progression

Rate of advancing through Bloom's cognitive levels per question attempted

Depth

Total Bloom's cognitive levels achieved (raw level-up count)

Breadth

Number of distinct learning points engaged with

Mastery

Accuracy in the student's most recent attempts (final quarter). High Accuracy + High Mastery = understood from the start. Lower Accuracy + High Mastery = understanding has been built over time.

Perseverance

Sustained commitment to practice — total questions attempted and active time invested, shown relative to the class. Check the Class Summary for absolute median and range values to judge the class baseline.

  • All-Round Excellence — strong across all six metrics; accurate, deep, broad, and demonstrating sustained perseverance
  • Persistent — high perseverance and wide coverage; accuracy and progression are the next areas to develop
  • Focused — very accurate in the areas they've covered; broadening coverage is the next step
  • Rising — recent mastery is high, showing real learning has taken place since earlier attempts
  • Early Stage — beginning their learning journey; even small steps forward will show up clearly on the radar
  • Specialist — gone deep into a small number of topics, reaching high cognitive levels
  • Explorer — sampled many topics; ready to choose a few and go deeper
  • Rebuilding — earlier accuracy shows genuine capability; recent focus is on consolidating and regaining momentum

Each student is matched to the two closest profiles. These are pattern suggestions, not diagnoses — your knowledge of the student's circumstances will always matter more than an algorithm.
